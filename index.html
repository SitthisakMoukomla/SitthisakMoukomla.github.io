About

I study the Earth from above — using satellite radar, LiDAR, and machine learning to measure forest carbon, map urban heat, and understand how tropical landscapes are changing.

I am a geospatial data scientist and Assistant Professor with a Ph.D. in Geography from the University of Colorado Boulder, combining roughly twenty years of operational satellite remote-sensing experience at GISTDA, Thailand's national space agency, with an active academic research program. My expertise spans statistical and machine-learning modeling of environmental data, synthetic aperture radar (SAR) and LiDAR analysis, surface energy-balance and land-surface temperature estimation, and large-scale geospatial computing on cloud platforms. I teach programming, geographic data science, and applied artificial intelligence for geographers.
